    10→
    11→# 1. Product
    12→
    13→A focused QR platform with exactly two core product features:
    14→
    15→1. Static QR
    16→2. Dynamic QR
    17→
    18→The product should feel like a tactile editorial design studio rather than a generic SaaS dashboard.
    19→
    20→Core product promise:
    21→
    22→> Static QR — Encode once.
    23→>
    24→> Dynamic QR — Change anytime. Measure every scan.
    25→
    26→The application must prioritize:
    27→
    28→- Simplicity
    29→- Scan reliability
    30→- Beautiful QR output
    31→- Fast creation
    32→- Dynamic destination editing
    33→- Useful scan analytics
    34→- Version history
    35→- Security
    36→- Responsive behavior
    37→
    38→Do NOT expand the product into a large QR marketplace or marketing automation platform.
    39→

   148→# 5. Static QR Definition
   149→
   150→Static QR encodes the supplied content directly into the QR.
   151→
   152→Example:
   153→
   154→```text
   155→User input
   156→    ↓
   157→https://example.com
   158→    ↓
   159→QR encoding
   160→    ↓
   161→Static QR
   162→```
   163→
   164→The QR does not require a platform redirect.
   165→
   166→The encoded content cannot be changed after generation.
   167→
   168→Static QR should be usable with:
   169→
   170→* URLs
   171→* Plain text
   172→* Other valid QR-compatible text content
   173→
   174→Do not build separate QR-type wizards for V1.
   175→
   176→---
   177→
   178→# 6. Dynamic QR Definition
   179→
   180→Dynamic QR contains a platform-controlled redirect URL.
   181→
   182→Example:
   183→
   184→```text
   185→QR
   186→ ↓
   187→qr.yourdomain.com/a8K29
   188→ ↓
   189→Redirect service
   190→ ↓
   191→Analytics event
   192→ ↓
   193→Current destination
   194→ ↓
   195→User destination
   196→```
   197→
   198→The destination can be changed without changing the printed QR.
   199→
   200→Dynamic QR requires an authenticated account.

  1417→
  1418→Never destroy historical destination records.
  1419→
  1420→Bad:
  1421→

  1442→```
  1443→
  1444→This preserves the audit trail.
