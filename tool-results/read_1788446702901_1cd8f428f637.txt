     3→# Product Requirements Document — Dynamic & Static QR Platform
     4→
     5→**Working product name:** QR Platform
     6→**Document version:** 1.0
     7→**Product type:** Web SaaS
     8→**Primary features:**
     9→
    10→1. Static QR Generator
    11→2. Dynamic QR Generator + Analytics
    12→
    13→**Core principle:**
    14→
    15→> **Static QR = generate once, works forever.**
    16→> **Dynamic QR = edit anytime, track every scan.**
    17→
    18→---
    19→
    20→# 1. Executive Summary
    21→
    22→The product is a web-based QR platform that allows users to create two kinds of QR codes:
    23→
    24→### Static QR
    25→
    26→The user's information is encoded directly into the QR code.
    27→
    28→```text
    29→User enters destination
    30→        ↓
    31→QR generated
    32→        ↓
    33→Information encoded directly
    34→        ↓
    35→Download / share
    36→```
    37→
    38→Once generated, the encoded information cannot be changed.
    39→
    40→### Dynamic QR
    41→
    42→The QR contains a platform-managed redirect URL.
    43→
    44→```text
    45→User enters destination
    46→        ↓
    47→Platform creates unique QR
    48→        ↓
    49→QR contains platform redirect
    50→        ↓
    51→User scans
    52→        ↓
    53→Platform records scan
    54→        ↓
    55→Platform resolves destination
    56→        ↓
    57→User redirected
    58→```
    59→
    60→The destination can subsequently be changed without reprinting the QR.
    61→
    62→---
    63→
    64→# 2. Product Vision
    65→
    66→> **Make QR creation extremely simple while making dynamic QR codes measurable, editable, reliable, and secure.**
    67→
    68→The product should not attempt to become:
    69→
    70→* A website builder
    71→* A marketing automation platform
    72→* A CRM
    73→* A payment platform
    74→* A social media tool
    75→* A huge QR-type marketplace
    76→
    77→The product has one job:
    78→
    79→> **Create QR codes and, for dynamic QR codes, give users control and useful analytics.**

   423→# 13. Feature 2 — Dynamic QR
   424→
   425→This is the primary SaaS feature.
   426→
   427→## Purpose
   428→
   429→Allow users to create a QR whose destination can be changed later while maintaining the same printed QR.

   432→
   433→# 14. Dynamic QR Architecture
   434→
   435→```text
   436→             QR CODE
   437→                │
   438→                ▼
   439→    qr.yourdomain.com/x8K29
   440→                │
   441→                ▼
   442→        Redirect Service
   443→                │
   444→        ┌───────┴────────┐
   445→        ▼                ▼
   446→   Record Scan       Resolve URL
   447→                         │
   448→                         ▼
   449→                    Destination
   450→```
   451→
   452→The actual destination should **never be encoded directly** into the dynamic QR.
   453→
   454→---
   455→
   456→# 15. Dynamic QR Creation
   457→
   458→User enters:
   459→
   460→### QR Name
   461→
   462→```text
   463→Summer Sale Poster
   464→```
   465→
   466→### Destination
   467→
   468→```text
   469→https://example.com/summer-sale
   470→```
   471→
   472→Then:
   473→
   474→**Create Dynamic QR**
   475→
   476→System generates:
   477→
   478→```text
   479→https://qr.yourdomain.com/x8K29
   480→```
   481→
   482→That URL is encoded into the QR.
   483→
   484→---
   485→
   486→# 16. Dynamic QR URL Requirements
   487→
   488→The generated identifier must be:
   489→
   490→* Unique
   491→* Non-sequential
   492→* Unpredictable
   493→* URL-safe
   494→* Short enough for practical QR encoding
   495→
   496→Example:
   497→
   498→```text
   499→qr.example.com/a8Kx92
   500→```
   501→
   502→Avoid:
   503→
   504→```text
   505→qr.example.com/12345
   506→```
   507→
   508→because predictable identifiers create unnecessary enumeration risk.
   509→
   510→---
   511→
   512→# 17. Dynamic QR Editing
   513→
   514→The user can edit:
   515→
   516→### Destination URL
   517→
   518→Before:
   519→
   520→```text
   521→https://example.com/offer
   522→```
   523→
   524→After:
   525→
   526→```text
   527→https://example.com/new-offer
   528→```
   529→
   530→The QR image remains unchanged.

   533→
   534→# 18. Dynamic QR Lifecycle
   535→
   536→Each dynamic QR has a state.
   537→
   538→```text
   539→ACTIVE
   540→   │
   541→   ├── EDIT
   542→   │
   543→   ├── PAUSE
   544→   │
   545→   ├── RESUME
   546→   │
   547→   └── ARCHIVE
   548→```
   549→
   550→### Active
   551→
   552→Redirect works normally.
   553→
   554→### Paused
   555→
   556→QR displays a configurable fallback message or platform-managed inactive page.
   557→
   558→### Archived
   559→
   560→QR is no longer actively managed.
