    13→    13→A focused QR platform with exactly two core product features:
    14→    14→
    15→    15→1. Static QR

    21→    21→

    23→    23→>
    24→    24→> Dynamic QR — Change anytime. Measure every scan.

    27→    27→
    28→    28→- Simplicity
    29→    29→- Scan reliability
    30→    30→- Beautiful QR output
    31→    31→- Fast creation
    32→    32→- Dynamic destination editing
    33→    33→- Useful scan analytics
    34→    34→- Version history
    35→    35→- Security
    36→    36→- Responsive behavior

   148→   148→# 5. Static QR Definition
   149→   149→
   150→   150→Static QR encodes the supplied content directly into the QR.
   151→   151→
   152→   152→Example:
   153→   153→
   154→   154→```text
   155→   155→User input
   156→   156→    ↓
   157→   157→https://example.com
   158→   158→    ↓
   159→   159→QR encoding
   160→   160→    ↓
   161→   161→Static QR
   162→   162→```
   163→   163→
   164→   164→The QR does not require a platform redirect.
   165→   165→
   166→   166→The encoded content cannot be changed after generation.
   167→   167→
   168→   168→Static QR should be usable with:
   169→   169→
   170→   170→* URLs
   171→   171→* Plain text
   172→   172→* Other valid QR-compatible text content
   173→   173→
   174→   174→Do not build separate QR-type wizards for V1.
   175→   175→
   176→   176→---
   177→   177→
   178→   178→# 6. Dynamic QR Definition
   179→   179→
   180→   180→Dynamic QR contains a platform-controlled redirect URL.
   181→   181→
   182→   182→Example:
   183→   183→
   184→   184→```text
   185→   185→QR
   186→   186→ ↓
   187→   187→qr.yourdomain.com/a8K29
   188→   188→ ↓
   189→   189→Redirect service
   190→   190→ ↓
   191→   191→Analytics event
   192→   192→ ↓
   193→   193→Current destination
   194→   194→ ↓
   195→   195→User destination
   196→   196→```
   197→   197→
   198→   198→The destination can be changed without changing the printed QR.
   199→   199→
   200→   200→Dynamic QR requires an authenticated account.
   201→   201→
